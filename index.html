What I work on

3 threads

Three questions I keep coming back to, from the object being studied to the incentive that decides whether anyone adopts the answer.

mechanistic

What is the structure?

Not just observing that a model works, but knowing why. Circuits, neurons, and the geometry of the features they compute.
autointerp

Does it scale?

Understanding models from the inside without a human in the loop for every feature. Approaches that get cheaper as models get bigger.
theory of change

Does it pay for itself?

Negative alignment taxes: safety properties that make a system more capable, not less, so labs adopt them because they work.

My north star is to help AGI go well. I think the most direct path runs through negative alignment taxes: safety that labs adopt because it works, not out of goodwill. Mechanistic understanding that scales is how we get there.
